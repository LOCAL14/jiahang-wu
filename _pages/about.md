I am **Jia-Hang Wu** (吴佳航; also indexed as Jiahang Wu / Wu Jia-Hang), a Ph.D. candidate at Southeast University working on **molecular ferroelectrics**, **hybrid metal / fluoroantimonite materials**, and **nonlinear optical (NLO)** crystals. My recent work focuses on engineering **Rashba–Dresselhaus spin splitting** in ferroelectric semiconductors for circularly polarized light (CPL) detection, and on designing materials with strong second-harmonic generation (SHG) and birefringence.

I am affiliated with the **Jiangsu Key Laboratory for Science and Applications of Molecular Ferroelectrics**, School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Southeast University, Nanjing, China.

**Research interests:** Nonlinear Optical Materials · Rashba Spin Splitting · Crystal Engineering · Semiconductor Materials / Molecular Ferroelectrics.
